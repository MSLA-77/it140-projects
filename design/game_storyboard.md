## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Escape the Haunted Hotel is a mystery and survival adventure game set inside a haunted hotel.

**Storyline:**

The player is trapped inside a haunted hotel and must find a way to escape. To survive, the player must explore the hotel and collect six items: a flashlight, key, rope, cell phone, security badge, and crowbar. These items are located in different rooms throughout the hotel. The player must collect all six items before entering the basement, where the Haunted Hotel Keeper is waiting. If the player encounters the Haunted Hotel Keeper before collecting all six items, the player loses the game. If all six items are collected first, the player wins and escapes the hotel.
## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1.  Hotel Lobby (Start room)
2.  Kitchen
3.  Dining Room
4.  Laundry Room
5.  Guest Room
6.  Security Room
7.  Storage Room
8.  Basement (Villain room)

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Flashlight
2. Key
3. Rope
4. Cell Phone
5. Security Badge
6. Crowbar

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The Haunted Hotel Keeper is the villain who waits in the basement. The player must avoid the Haunted Hotel Keeper until all six items have been collected. If the player enters the basement before collecting all six items, the player loses the game.
